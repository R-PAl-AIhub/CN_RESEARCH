\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
DDoS floods are among the most damaging threats to IoT deployments, and federated learning (FL)~\cite{mcmahan2017,kairouz2021} lets gateways train a shared detector without pooling traffic. Karunamurthy et al.\ pair a chimp-optimisation feature-selection wrapper with a federated CNN on MQTTset~\cite{karunamurthy2025}; a follow-up (the ``senior'' work) replaces both by a Pearson--LDA intersection filter and a federated linear SVM~\cite{cortes1995} on CIC-IoT2023~\cite{senior2026}, a lightweight design aimed at constrained gateways.

Our audit (Section~\ref{sec:audit}) shows that its privacy argument stops at the model: the variance filter, Pearson and LDA selectors and the scaler are fitted on \emph{pooled} data \emph{before} the train/test split, so raw flows must be centralised and test statistics leak; the ``rounds'' retrain every worker from scratch (one-shot averaging); and duplicate flows can straddle the split. Federated preprocessing has been studied only in pieces: StatAvg shares client feature means and variances to normalise deep FL-IDS models~\cite{bouzinis2025}, and chi-square or mutual-information selection has been paired with deep models~\cite{nguyen2026,fdids2025}. To the best of our knowledge, no work derives a hybrid Pearson-and-LDA subset \emph{and} global standardisation exactly from additive client statistics for a linear detector, quantifies what pooled pre-split preprocessing leaks, and tests naive local alternatives under non-IID clients. We ask:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{RQ1.} Can the selection and standardisation be computed exactly from additive client statistics, and how much optimism does pooled pre-split preprocessing add over a leakage-free protocol?
\item \textbf{RQ2.} How much do naive local preprocessing pipelines lose, versus federated-statistics preprocessing, as clients become non-IID?
\item \textbf{RQ3.} What does the statistics exchange cost, and does the lightweight detector stay competitive with a federated MLP, FedProx and a centralised random forest?
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Contributions.} (1) A reproduction and audit of the senior pipeline on real CIC-IoT2023 flows (twelve defects). (2) \emph{FedStat-FS}, which extends StatAvg from normalisation to the whole preprocessing stage, followed by warm-start FedAvg of a TensorFlow linear SVM. (3) A leakage-free, de-duplicated, ten-seed protocol with identical tuning budgets on two real datasets, one with a natural per-device federation. (4) Empirical findings, including negative ones.
