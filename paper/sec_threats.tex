\subsection{Threats to Validity and Limitations}
\textbf{Internal.} Hyper-parameters were chosen on validation splits of corpora 0--2, whose pool rows can reappear in other seeds' test splits (all corpora share one de-duplicated pool); with six candidates per method the optimism is negligible but not zero. A failure is a run with test F1 $<0.9$ (descriptive). The federation is simulated on one machine, so timings exclude communication. The base pipeline~\cite{karunamurthy2025} is our re-implementation of an under-specified method; with our fitness weight (0.99) it kept far fewer features than the 17 of 34 it reports. \textbf{External.} Two testbed datasets, binary tasks, 9--10 clients; Dirichlet partitions are synthetic \emph{partitions} of real flows, the N-BaIoT device split is natural. \textbf{Construct.} Privacy is argued structurally and not measured (no secure aggregation or differential privacy); ``leakage'' is judged only by the performance gap, which selection variability confounds. \textbf{Limitations.} The CPU budget fixed 20 rounds, 2 local epochs, six tuning trials and the corpus sizes of Table~\ref{tab:datasets} for all methods; the pseudo-inverse of $S_W$ costs $O(d^3)$ at the server (negligible for $d\le115$); stragglers, malicious clients and multi-class typing were not studied; the pooled forest is a non-federated ceiling, not a competitor.
